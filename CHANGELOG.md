Changes since 6.2.1 (`Beta-6.2.1-b134`).

- Improved background upload reliability and handling of upload task expiration
- Corrected background colors in information sheets
- Improved close buttons and their accessibility labels

### Changed

- Updated Tuist to 4.211.0 and refreshed Swift package dependencies
